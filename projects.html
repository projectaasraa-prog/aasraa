Accessibility Statement

Project Aasraa is committed to digital accessibility for everyone. This site is designed for WCAG-aligned colour contrast, full keyboard navigation, screen-reader-friendly structure, descriptive alt text, and reduced-motion support. If you encounter any barrier, please tell us via our form — we'll make it right.
