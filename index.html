Our Story

About Project Aasraa

Project Aasraa began as a simple conviction shared by two students: that dignity should never depend on ability or gender. What started as conversations about the barriers we saw around us grew into a youth-led organisation working at the intersection of disability inclusion and gender equality.

We listen first. We partner with persons with disabilities, women, caregivers and communities to co-create awareness, access and opportunity — turning empathy into measurable change.

“Aasraa” means support and hope.

A shelter to lean on, and the belief that tomorrow can be more inclusive than today. That meaning guides everything we build.

Our Journey

A timeline of hope

  1. The Spark

    Two students begin documenting accessibility gaps in their community and ask, “Why not us?”

  2. Project Aasraa is born

    Our first awareness drives on disability inclusion & gender equality reach local schools.

  3. Growing the circle

    Volunteers, NGOs and educators join — programs expand into workshops and advocacy.

  4. Today & beyond

    A growing movement building inclusive, equal communities — one voice at a time.

What Drives Us

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

A society where inclusion and equality are not ideals but everyday realities — where every person with a disability and every woman lives with dignity, access and opportunity.

Our Mission

To advance disability inclusion, accessibility and gender equality through advocacy, education and community-led action — amplifying voices that have too often gone unheard.

Our Programs

What We Do

Six focus areas, one shared goal: communities where everyone belongs.

Disability Inclusion & Advocacy

Championing rights, representation and barrier-free participation for persons with disabilities.

Accessibility Awareness

Spreading practical know-how so public spaces, schools and the web work for everyone.

Gender Equality

Empowering women and girls with voice, safety and equal opportunity to lead.

Community Education

Workshops and drives that turn awareness into empathy and everyday inclusive habits.

Leadership Development

Nurturing young changemakers with the skills and confidence to lead inclusion forward.

Partnerships for Social Impact

Collaborating with NGOs, schools and corporates to scale inclusion sustainably.

Why Our Work Matters

Behind every statistic is a person waiting to be included.

Persons with disabilities and women too often face barriers to education, employment, safety and basic dignity — not because of ability, but because of how the world is built. We're working to change that, together.
